Disaster Recovery Plan

The disaster recovery plan provides basic actions that the IT department can follow when a serious IT problem occurs.

Situation Recovery Action
Server failure Restore the server and recover the latest verified backup.
Hardware failure Replace the failed equipment with available spare equipment.
Internet outage Contact the ISP and use an alternative connection where available.
Power failure Use UPS equipment and safely shut down critical systems.
Cyberattack Isolate affected devices and begin security recovery procedures.
Data loss Restore information from the latest verified backup.
Fire or flood Protect staff first and activate the company's recovery arrangements.

Priority

The first priority during a disaster is to protect employees. Once the situation is safe, the IT department can assess the damage and restore important services.
