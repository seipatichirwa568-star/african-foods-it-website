IT Security Policy

The IT Security Policy provides employees with basic rules for protecting company systems and information.

Employees Must:

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Keep passwords private.
  • Lock computers when leaving their workstation.
  • Report suspicious emails or files.
  • Only access information required for their work.
  • Keep company devices physically secure.
  • Report lost or stolen devices to the IT department.
  • Install required security updates.
  • Use approved company software.
  • Follow backup and data protection procedures.

Employees Must Not:

  • Share passwords with other employees.
  • Install unauthorised software.
  • Connect unknown devices to the company network.
  • Attempt to bypass security controls.
  • Access another employee's information without permission.
  • Use company equipment for illegal activities.

Purpose

These rules help reduce the risk of unauthorised access, malware infections and loss of company information.
