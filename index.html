A few habits that make a difference

  • Lock your screen. Protect information when you leave your desk. On a Windows computer, press Windows + L.
  • Keep passwords private. Do not include them in support reports or share them with colleagues.
  • Report problems clearly. Include your branch, the affected device, the error and the effect on your work.
  • Check before buying. Equipment purchases need the required technical, budget and management approvals.

Frequently asked questions

Can I submit a support ticket through this website?

No. This version explains how to prepare and report a problem. It does not send information to an IT team or create a live support ticket. Use the company's approved reporting channel.

Who approves a new computer?

Your manager confirms the need, IT assesses suitability and Finance checks the budget. Company management gives final purchasing approval before an order is placed.

Read the full procedure →
What should I do if the whole branch loses access?

Report the branch-wide impact through an available approved channel. The branch contact should coordinate reporting when normal network access is unavailable. IT will assess the fault and arrange the appropriate escalation.

Read the help desk guidance →
Does completing a checklist approve a request?

No. The checklists help you prepare or review your understanding. They do not approve purchases, submit acknowledgements or confirm that recovery actions have been performed.

Where can I find the maintenance responsibilities?

The maintenance page identifies proposed daily, weekly, monthly and quarterly checks, as well as tasks triggered by changes or staff movements.
