IT Equipment Acquisition Procedure

When new IT equipment is required, employees should follow the company's equipment acquisition procedure.

Procedure

  1. Identify the equipment requirement.
  2. Submit the request to the IT department.
  3. IT checks the technical requirements.
  4. Obtain quotations from suitable suppliers.
  5. The IT Manager reviews the request.
  6. Finance checks the available budget.
  7. Management approves the purchase.
  8. A purchase order is created.
  9. The equipment is delivered.
  10. IT configures and tests the equipment.
  11. The equipment is recorded in the asset register.

Purpose

This procedure helps prevent unnecessary purchases and makes sure that new equipment is suitable for the existing network.
