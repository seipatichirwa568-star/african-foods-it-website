Acceptable Use Policy

This policy explains how employees should use company computers, internet access, email and other IT resources.

Employees Should:

  • Use company IT resources mainly for work activities.
  • Use approved websites and online services.
  • Use company email professionally.
  • Be careful when opening unknown links and attachments.
  • Keep company information confidential.
  • Report suspicious activity to the IT department.

Employees Should Not:

  • Use company systems for illegal activities.
  • Install unauthorised software.
  • Attempt to access systems without permission.
  • Download harmful software.
  • Attack or damage other computer systems.
  • Share company passwords.

Monitoring

The IT department may monitor company systems when necessary to maintain security and make sure IT resources are being used appropriately.
